SAFETY BASICS

Small habits can
prevent big problems.

01

Stay aware

Notice your surroundings, especially when travelling, using unfamiliar places or dealing with unexpected situations.

02

Don't panic

Pause, assess the situation and decide what needs to happen next. Panic can make a manageable situation harder.

03

Get help early

If a situation is dangerous, contact emergency services or a trusted person instead of trying to handle everything alone.

04

Know your exits

In unfamiliar buildings or public spaces, notice exits and emergency routes before you actually need them.

EMERGENCY RESPONSE

When seconds matter, clarity matters too.

In an emergency, first make sure you are safe enough to help. Then communicate the situation clearly and give responders the information they need.

01

Move away from danger

If possible, get yourself and others to a safer location.

02

Call for help

Contact the appropriate emergency service and explain what happened.

03

Share your location

Give a clear address, landmark or other location information.

04

Follow instructions

Listen to emergency responders and avoid unnecessary risks.

EVERYDAY SAFETY

Think ahead.
React better.

🔥

Fire safety

Know your exits, avoid blocking escape routes and never ignore smoke or fire alarms.

🚦

Road safety

Stay alert around traffic, follow signals and avoid using your phone while crossing or driving.

🏠

Home safety

Check electrical connections, keep hazardous items away from children and know where important switches are.

🚶

Personal safety

Trust your instincts, stay aware of your surroundings and let someone know when plans change unexpectedly.

KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

Knowing your rights
changes how you respond.

You don't need to be a legal expert. Start by knowing where to look for reliable information and when to ask for help.

01

Consumer awareness

Keep bills, receipts, warranties and important communication. Read terms before agreeing to purchases or services.

02

Digital fraud

If you suspect a digital payment scam, act quickly, preserve transaction details and use official reporting channels.

03

Privacy

Think carefully before sharing personal information. Check why information is being requested and who will receive it.

04

Official information

For legal, financial or government matters, prefer official government sources over forwarded messages and random social-media posts.
