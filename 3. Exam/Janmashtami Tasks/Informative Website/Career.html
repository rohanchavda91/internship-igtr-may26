LIFEOS / 03 — CAREER

Build a career you can grow with.

A career is more than getting your first job. Learn how to present yourself, communicate clearly, prepare for interviews and keep developing useful skills.

CAREER FORMULA Learn. Build. Grow.
START HERE

Your career is a long game.

You don't need to have everything figured out. Focus on building useful skills, gaining experience and learning how to communicate your value.

01

Know yourself

Understand your strengths, interests, preferred work style and the skills you want to develop.

02

Build skills

Combine technical knowledge with communication, problem-solving and teamwork skills.

03

Show your work

Projects, portfolios, internships and practical experience can demonstrate what you can actually do.

04

Keep learning

Technologies and workplaces change. Treat learning as an ongoing part of your career.

RESUME

Make your resume easy to understand.

A good resume quickly communicates who you are, what you know and what you have accomplished. Keep it relevant to the opportunity.

01

Clear contact information

Use professional contact details and make important information easy to find.

02

Relevant skills

Highlight skills that actually relate to the role you're applying for.

03

Projects & experience

Describe what you built or accomplished, not just what your responsibilities were.

04

Proofread

Spelling, formatting and consistency matter because they affect readability.

COMMUNICATION

Say what you mean, clearly.

Strong communication isn't about using complicated words. It's about making your message easy to understand.

01

Listen first

Understand the other person's point before preparing your response.

02

Be specific

Clear details reduce confusion and unnecessary back-and-forth.

03

Stay professional

Be respectful even when you disagree or need to communicate difficult information.

INTERVIEWS

Preparation beats panic.

Research the organization, understand the role and prepare examples that demonstrate your skills.

01 Research the role
02 Prepare examples
03 Practice clearly
04 Ask thoughtful questions
WORKPLACE

Professionalism is a habit.

01

Be reliable

Respect deadlines and communicate early if something is going wrong.

02

Take feedback

Feedback is information that can help you improve your work.

03

Respect boundaries

Professional relationships work better when expectations and boundaries are respected.

04

Own your work

Take responsibility for your decisions, mistakes and contributions.

INTERACTIVE

Interview quick check.

Test your interview instincts.

QUESTION 01

What is the best response when you don't know the answer to an interview question?
