LIFEOS / 01 — MONEY

Understand money before it controls you.

Money affects almost every part of adult life. You don't need to be a financial expert — but understanding the basics can help you make better everyday decisions.

THE BASICS Earn. Manage. Grow.
START HERE

Your money has a job.

Before thinking about investments or complicated financial products, understand where your money comes from and where it goes.

01

Earn

Income is the money you receive from work, business, freelancing, investments or other legitimate sources.

02

Spend

Spending covers everything from essentials such as food and transport to entertainment and lifestyle choices.

03

Save

Saving means setting aside money instead of spending it immediately, especially for future needs and unexpected situations.

04

Grow

Over time, appropriate investments can help your money grow. Investing always involves some level of risk.

SMART SPENDING

Need it or want it?

One of the simplest financial habits is learning to distinguish between something you genuinely need and something you simply want.

NEED

Essential

  • Food
  • Basic housing
  • Essential transport
  • Healthcare
WANT

Optional

  • Entertainment
  • Luxury upgrades
  • Impulse purchases
  • Non-essential subscriptions
BUDGETING

Give every rupee a purpose.

A budget is simply a plan for how you intend to use your income. It doesn't have to be complicated.

01

Know your income

Start with the money you actually expect to receive during the month.

02

List your expenses

Separate regular essentials from flexible and optional spending.

03

Leave some room

Don't plan every rupee to be spent. Unexpected expenses happen.

SAVING

Build a cushion for tomorrow.

Savings can help you handle planned purchases, emergencies and periods when income is uncertain.

01

Emergency fund

Keep accessible savings for unexpected expenses such as urgent repairs, medical needs or temporary loss of income.

02

Short-term goals

Save separately for upcoming goals such as education expenses, travel or a planned purchase.

03

Long-term goals

Long-term financial goals require consistency, patience and a plan that matches your situation.

BANKING BASICS

Know the tools you use.

Savings Account

A bank account designed for holding money while generally allowing withdrawals and transfers.

FD

A fixed deposit generally keeps money deposited for a chosen period in exchange for interest.

Loan

Borrowed money that must generally be repaid according to agreed terms, usually with interest.

UPI

A digital payment system that enables instant bank-to-bank payments through supported apps.

WATCH OUT

Common money mistakes.

01

Spending without knowing where your money goes.

02

Buying things impulsively because of discounts.

03

Ignoring the cost of borrowing money.

04

Putting all your financial decisions off until later.
