LIFEOS / 02 — DIGITAL

Be smart online. Stay safe offline.

The internet is incredibly useful — and scammers know that too. Learn how to protect your accounts, recognize suspicious activity and make smarter decisions online.

DIGITAL RULE Pause. Verify. Then act.
THE BASICS

Your digital life needs protection.

A few simple habits can significantly reduce the chances of losing access to an account or falling for a common online scam.

01

Protect your passwords

Use long, unique passwords for important accounts. Avoid reusing the same password everywhere.

02

Enable 2-step verification

Multi-factor authentication adds another layer of protection when someone attempts to access your account.

03

Keep software updated

Updates often include security fixes. Keeping your operating system and apps current reduces avoidable risks.

04

Think before clicking

Unexpected links, attachments and urgent requests deserve extra attention before you interact with them.

PHISHING

Don't take the bait.

Phishing is a type of fraud where someone tries to trick you into revealing information or clicking something dangerous by pretending to be a trusted person or organization.

01

Urgency

“Act immediately or your account will be blocked.”

02

Suspicious links

The displayed name may look familiar while the actual destination is different.

03

Unexpected requests

Be cautious when someone suddenly asks for passwords, OTPs or financial details.

04

Too good to be true

Unexpected prizes, refunds or job offers can be used as bait.

DIGITAL PAYMENTS

Your PIN approves the transaction.

Understanding how digital payments work can help you avoid common social-engineering tricks.

₹

Receiving money

Receiving money normally does not require you to enter your UPI PIN.

!

Never share your PIN

Your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, password and OTP should remain private.

⌁

Verify requests

Check the recipient and transaction details before approving a payment request.

PRIVACY

Your data is valuable.

Every app, website and service may collect different kinds of information. Pay attention to what you share and who you share it with.

01

Think before posting

Information shared publicly can be copied, downloaded or reshared.

02

Review permissions

Apps don't necessarily need access to every permission available on your device.

03

Lock your devices

Use a secure screen lock and keep important devices updated.

04

Question unexpected requests

Don't provide personal information simply because a message asks for it.

AI AWARENESS

Not everything you see is real.

AI-generated images, audio and video can look convincing. Before believing or sharing unusual content, check the source and look for independent confirmation.

01 Check the source
02 Look for context
03 Verify elsewhere
